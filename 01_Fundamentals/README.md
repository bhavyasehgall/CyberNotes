# Cybersecurity Fundamentals

The **Fundamentals** module is the starting point of the **CyberNotes** repository. It introduces the core concepts, principles, and terminology used throughout cybersecurity. Before learning networking, cryptography, ethical hacking, or security operations, it is essential to understand these foundational topics.

This module is designed for:

- Beginners starting their cybersecurity journey
- Students preparing for certifications
- SOC Analyst aspirants
- Penetration Testers
- Security Engineers
- Anyone who wants a strong foundation in cybersecurity

---

# Learning Objectives

After completing this module, you will be able to:

- Understand what cybersecurity is and why it is important.
- Explain the CIA Triad and other security principles.
- Differentiate between threats, vulnerabilities, and risks.
- Identify different types of hackers.
- Recognize common cyberattacks.
- Understand security controls and defense strategies.
- Explain authentication, authorization, and the AAA model.
- Understand Identity and Access Management (IAM).
- Explain Defense in Depth and Zero Trust.
- Understand the Cyber Kill Chain and MITRE ATT&CK Framework.
- Perform basic risk management concepts.
- Understand major cybersecurity frameworks.
- Explain organizational security policies, standards, procedures, guidelines, and baselines.
